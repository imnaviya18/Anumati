# Role & Workspace Guides — ANUMATI v2 (Sheet 01, APH Annexure-16)

## Workspaces (Sheet 01)
1. **Institution Workspace (`/institution`):** Submit/import applications (A1), view deficiency findings and submit responses (B2), upload undertaking compliance evidence and document renewals (E1, F3).
2. **Officer / Committee Workspace (`/officer`):** Open assigned stages (B1), record scrutiny/re-scrutiny findings (B2), manage EVC readiness, slot reservation, confirmation, visit and report (G1–G5), record SHC/SAC hearing outcomes, verify compliance/renewal evidence (E3–E4, F4), and record Competent Authority dispositions (C1–C2).
3. **Oversight / Audit Workspace (`/oversight`):** Monitor regional and bureau pendency, ageing against policy timelines, workload distribution, active escalations, complete case history, and export the six statutory CSV reports (J6, PS Section F).
4. **Policy & Administration Workspace (`/policy`, `/admin`):**
   - Policy Governance (`POLICY_ADMIN`): Draft, validate, dry-run, approve, and activate versioned policies (J1–J3).
   - System & Operations Administration (`TECHNICAL_ADMIN` / `OPS_ADMIN`): Manage users, roles, regions, bureaus, committees, assignments, outbox exceptions, and audited replays (J4–J5). Holds **zero regulatory authority** (Sheet 07 P5).
